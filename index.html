FAQ

Do I need a paid filter app?

No. We use Shopify Search & Discovery with metafields + minimal JS. Fast and no monthly fee.

Can my team add new SKUs later?

Yes. I’ll provide a short guide + CSV template so your metafields stay consistent.

Will it work on mobile?

Absolutely. We optimize images, use lightweight components, and test on real devices.

Can we show CAD/datasheets?

Yes, per product. We’ll add a “Downloads” block in the PDP metafields.
